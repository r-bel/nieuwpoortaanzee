The apartment

Centrally located in Nieuwpoort-Bad, 200 m from the beach, this bright apartment with garage is for sale. It makes an ideal base as a holiday home, an investment or a permanent residence.

The apartment has always been maintained with great care and is sold furnished. There is no renovation obligation (EPC label C), so you can enjoy it straight away and modernise later at your own pace.
